The role

Sales first. Support always.

You will work with Israeli travelers planning trips abroad. Most conversations happen through WhatsApp and are managed in Crisp, together with our AI bot and internal tour-management application.

Sales

  • Turn incoming leads into bookings
  • Recommend the right tour for the customer
  • Follow up on open opportunities
  • Handle availability, pricing and payment questions

Customer Support

  • Help existing customers before their tour
  • Handle changes, exceptions and cancellations correctly
  • Make sure confirmations and attendance are clear
  • Keep customers from falling between the cracks

Operations

  • Work with bookings and tours in our application
  • Check upcoming tours and operational gaps
  • Coordinate when something needs human attention
  • Keep records clean and accurate

AI + Judgment

  • Work alongside our AI bot
  • Take over conversations when the bot gets stuck
  • Flag incorrect bot behavior
  • Use judgment instead of blindly following scripts
A normal shift

What your work actually looks like.

Opening scan

Check the inbox, urgent conversations, new bookings and anything handed over from the previous shift.

Sales & support

Reply to customers, close bookings, solve issues and move open conversations forward.

Follow-up

Return to customers who need another touch, confirmation, availability check or payment step.

Operational check

Make sure relevant bookings and upcoming tours are correctly reflected in the system.

Clean handoff

Finish with a clear Shift Summary so the next person knows exactly what remains open.

The handoff

No conversation should disappear between shifts.

At the end of every shift, you complete a short Shift Summary. This is a core part of the job, not optional admin.

Shift Summary should include:

  • Customers still waiting for an answer
  • Open sales opportunities
  • Follow-ups that still need to happen
  • Payment issues
  • Special cases and exceptions
  • Anything the next shift must know
Who fits this role

You will probably fit if you…

Training

You will not be thrown into the inbox alone.

The onboarding starts with our company, Paris and our tours, then moves into our internal application and Crisp. You will read real conversations, complete knowledge checkpoints, and then work live while we stay in the background with you.

We start with Paris because it is one of our busiest destinations. If you can understand the variety there, adding smaller destinations becomes much easier.
